02 / Bio

FROM THE OTHER HALF OF UNITY COMMITTEE

In the early '90s, the LA crew Unity Committee split in two. One half became Jurassic 5. The other went darker and became Darkleaf. Longevity was one of its two producer-lyricists, building the beats and writing the bars alongside Jahli, with Kemit and Metalogik on the mic and Mixmaster Wolf (also of Breakestra) on the decks. They came out of the Good Life Cafe era, the Crenshaw open mic that came before Blowed.

He also came up through Project Blowed, the Thursday-night open-mic workshop at KAOS Network in Leimert Park, started in 1994 by Aceyalone and Abstract Rude. It was where LA MCs went to get tested, and where weak verses got cut off mid-bar.

After self-released records and a spot on the Beneath The Surface compilation, Darkleaf dropped F… The People on Ubiquity in 2002. It was dense and disorienting, with swirling rhythms and rhymes that made people uneasy, and it carried the crew's sound past the LA underground.

Longevity kept going: as one half of the production duo Lodge Infinite with Jahli, and solo, working with LA heads like 2Mex.

His latest, Audio Cinema, came out on Alpha Pup Records: nine tracks you watch with your ears, with MF DOOM and Prince Po on "Street Strangers", Alph Tha Alien on "Holy G", and "The Mask" produced by 3EAS.

He runs his own production company, LA TRAFFIC. Up next: Black on Black Crime, a full project with King of Low End Camp.

03 / Lineage

SIGNAL PATH

  1. EARLY '90s

    Unity Committee splits. Jurassic 5 on one side, Darkleaf on the other. Good Life Cafe era, Crenshaw.

  2. MID '90s

    Project Blowed, Thursday nights at KAOS Network, Leimert Park. The open mic that made LA's underground MCs.

  3. 1997

    Darkleaf records Kimetic Principles, with Lodge Infinite (Jahli & Longevity) producing.

  4. 1999

    First official appearance on the Beneath The Surface compilation.

  5. 2002

    F… The People on Ubiquity (URLP099). Longevity has vocal credits on "Commercial" and "Triple Stages". Kimetic Principles II follows.

  6. 2003

    Darkleaf featured on Existereo's Dirty Deeds & Dead Flowers.

  7. 2008

    Credited on The Game Manual 9.0.

  8. 2019

    Vulgar presses Kimetic Principles to vinyl for the first time, with Cawzaone artwork and an interview with Longevity in the insert.

  9. SOLO

    "No Surrender" featuring 2Mex. Debut solo album: Warrior Music.

  10. RECENT

    Audio Cinema on Alpha Pup Records. MF DOOM, Prince Po, Alph Tha Alien. "The Mask" prod. 3EAS.

  11. NOW

    In the lab: Black on Black Crime with King (Low End Camp), under his production company LA TRAFFIC.
