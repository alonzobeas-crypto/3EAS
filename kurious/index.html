[01] DOSSIER

Origin file

Subject Jorge Antonio Alvarez grew up in Manhattan Valley, the stretch just below Harlem on the West Side. He's Boricua and Cuban, and his mother built a life from nothing.

Rock Steady Park and the Zulu Nation were close by. At Game Night in the community room, the older kids rapped and cut breaks. That's where the signal started.

He interned at Def Jam and crossed paths with 3rd Bass. That's how he met Zev Love X of KMD, the man who later became DOOM, and they stayed creative partners for life. His peers gave him the name the Majician. Three decades later, the transmission is still running.

Two islands

1849SOMETHING IS WOVEN INTO THIS SIGNAL1895
Boricua y cubano.

In 1895, Puerto Rican exiles in Manhattan unveiled a new flag for their island: Cuba's design with the colors flipped. Two islands, one fight against the same empire, stitched together in New York.

Kurious carries both. His mother came from Puerto Rico at 13, an orphan who didn't speak English, and built a life uptown. Manhattan Valley in those days was Black and Puerto Rican, all mixed together, and the culture came out of that mix.
