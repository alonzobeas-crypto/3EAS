La Historia

From the cubicle to the crates.

Anthony Saldana worked inside the music business for years, from a desk. He saw how records got made, sold and pushed, then went after the part he loved.

I.
The Cubicle

An industry job behind a desk, close to the music without touching it.

II.
The Crates

He started DJing on vinyl: wax only, digging and learning every record by hand.

III.
The Booth

Now he's DJ Antiq, one of the names LA knows. Fifty years old, still digging.

Fashion runs in the familia.

His cousin owned T&A Showroom, so Anthony grew up around racks, samples and people who took clothes seriously. He loves color, he loves fashion, and he dresses with intent. SUCIOPATH is where that finally gets its own label.
