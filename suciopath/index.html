SUCIO
IS A
METHOD.

SUCIOPATH comes from the booth, not the boardroom. Every graphic starts as a flyer, a crate, a burned CD-R with the label half rubbed off. It gets printed on heavy cotton and sent out into the street.

  1. Small runs. Each drop is numbered. When it's gone, it's gone.
  2. Heavyweight blanks. Boxy fit, made to take a beating.
  3. Archive resale. Vintage band, rave and LA tees, found and checked.
  4. Sound first. Each drop comes with a mix. Wear the tee, play the tape.
